the principle

The method never modularizes.
The container does.

A menu of modules would let every buyer skip the domains that make the work real, which is the deck's own slide 3 antagonist. What founders are asking for is not curriculum choice. It is commitment shape: the size of the first yes, the cash-flow form, the delivery terrain. Each rung of the ladder sells more of one scarce asset: how much of the client's shape Jeremy takes on.

the credit law · one rule, no exceptions

Personal money buying personal work credits forward. Company money does not.

the hours law · shapes, never banked

52 banked hours turns a floor into a ceiling. Time is sold as shapes; the spine is never metered.
