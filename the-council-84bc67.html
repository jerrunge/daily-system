The Council

Every serious company has a board. Almost no person does. This is a standing board of directors for people building alone, and it never adjourns.

The gap

Builders alone get no governance

A funded founder gets an exec team: a finance mind watching runway, a marketing mind shaping the story, a board asking the questions nobody inside the work can see. A person building alone gets a group chat. Masterminds meet monthly and evaporate. Coaches bill by the hour and leave. Advice arrives, and nothing holds it, nothing follows up, nothing remembers.

The gap was never about willingness. Three friends who want to govern each other's success have had no instrument for it, because the staff work underneath a real board, the briefing, the pattern reading, the follow-through, took salaries nobody building alone can pay. That constraint just died.

The invention

The staff layer

Each member seats an AI chief of staff. It holds their whole picture: how the ten domains feel this week, the goals, the moves in flight, where they are stuck. Instruments a member already runs can feed it; a notebook and a weekly conversation feed it just as well.

And the staffs meet while the humans sleep.

The cycle

One cycle, running forever

  1. 1Gauge. Each member marks how the ten domains feel this week. Felt, not scored. Two minutes.
  2. 2Publish. Each chooses what crosses the membrane, signal by signal. The rest stays home.
  3. 3Confer. The staffs meet. Patterns read across every seat, needs matched to strengths, asks drafted.
  4. 4The packet. One page per chair, the connective work already done. Nobody arrives cold.
  5. 5The table. The humans sit. Judgment, care, rulings. The system never votes.
  6. 6Land. Rulings return to each life as scheduled moves, into whatever instrument that member runs. The staffs watch the follow-through.

then it turns over, and the board keeps sitting.

The charter

Together, and separate is the architecture

Sovereignty here is not a settings page. It is the load-bearing wall.

I

Your data lives with you. The table sees only what you publish, signal by signal, week by week.

II

The system briefs. It never rules. Humans hold every gavel.

III

No score of a person exists anywhere in it. Gauges are felt and self-set. Verdicts about people are forbidden by design.

IV

It may choose what to lead with. It never decides what you are allowed to see, and it never assesses you without telling you.

V

Floors, not ceilings. It amplifies what a member already carries. It never caps, chaperones, or shames.

One of the founders already wrote these laws for himself, refusal by refusal, in a system of record. Here they become the charter, for every member. They are also the moat: a product that harvests three lives into one database cannot retrofit trust. One built on consent from the first commit can hold strangers.
