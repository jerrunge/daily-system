The Arsenal

What to actually do, per domain, across the keystones, and in the ten situations that bring people here. Every practice carries the evidence standing under it and the trap that looks like a move.

Evidence View

The keystone protocols

what crosses two domains
PH + ME · Mind-Body Unity

The paired week

Every psychological intervention gets a physical prescription in the same seven days. Combined work measures nearly double either one alone. This is the shape of the engagement, not an addition to it.

T1 · pooled · SMD 0.73
Wilkie L, Fisher Z, Geidel A, et al. Nat Hum Behav 2026;10:715-726. doi:10.1038/s41562-025-02369-1. 183 RCTs, 22,811 participants. Sex was not tested as a moderator, so no sex-specific version of this is available in either direction.
SX + RL · Intimacy Spillover

The dyad is the unit

When he is partnered, intimate or withdrawal work is never scoped to him alone, and the partner's outcome is a tracked outcome. Treat one and the other improves without being treated.

T1 · causal
Heiman JR, et al. BJOG 2007;114(4):437-447. doi:10.1111/j.1471-0528.2006.01228.x. Multicentre double-blind placebo-controlled RCT, P<0.0001. Supporting: Chew PY, et al. J Sex Med 2021;18(1):99-112, OR 3.011 (95% CI 1.856-4.885).
FI + ID · Identity Fusion

Affirm before deciding

Compensation becomes a verdict on the self. Run values affirmation immediately before a consequential financial or career decision rather than after it.

T2 · sequencing is inference
Zhang Y, Chen B, Hu X, Wang M. Am Psychol 2025. doi:10.1037/amp0001591. 129 tests from 67 articles. The affirmation evidence is real; that it protects financial decision quality specifically has not been tested.
PH + SX · Depletion Cascade

Parallel pressures, not a chain

Three pressures arrive together: sleep debt, mood, and demand. Overwork reaches sexual function through sleep, stress and attention rather than through a blood panel. It is still not a character flaw.

T1 · pooled
Mechanism: Hamilton LD, Meston CM. J Sex Med 2013;10:2443-2454. doi:10.1111/jsm.12249, where the predictor of reduced genital arousal was cognitive distraction. The single-chain version through testosterone is contradicted: Eddy P, et al. Psychosom Med 2023;85(5):450-460; Davis SR, et al. JCEM 2019;104(10):4660-4666.

By domain

tap to open

T1 is a meta-analysis of randomized trials or a large trial measuring the outcome directly. T2 is a single trial or consistent prospective cohort evidence. Where a finding is observational the wording says "associated with" and never "produces."

PHPhysicalWhat the body can do, and what it costs to keep doing it.4›

The activity floor, aimed at the bottom of the curve

T1 · pooled

Do: the prescription for a sedentary executive is not 150 minutes. It is the first 30. Move him off zero, then build.

The dose-response curve is inverse and curvilinear, with the steepest gradient at the lowest activity volumes, and real benefit below the public health recommendation. Most of the return sits in the first increment.

Pearce M, Garcia L, Abbas A, et al. JAMA Psychiatry 2022;79:550-559. doi:10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2022.0609. 15 studies, 191,130 participants, 2,110,588 person-years. Pooled cohort data, so say "associated with."

Combined physical and psychological work in the same week

T1 · SMD 0.73

Do: never run a session without a physical prescription attached to the same week.

Combined interventions measured 0.73 against 0.41 to 0.49 for exercise alone or mindfulness alone, and 0.37 to 0.39 for ACT alone.

Wilkie L, et al. Nat Hum Behav 2026;10:715-726. doi:10.1038/s41562-025-02369-1.

The fourteen-day sleep-opportunity audit

T1 · causal

Do: fourteen days of time-in-bed logging with an objective performance check, never a self-rating.

Fourteen days at six hours in bed produced deficits equivalent to two nights of total sleep deprivation, while subjective sleepiness plateaued and performance kept falling. He cannot feel it, which is the whole point.

Van Dongen HP, Maislin G, Mullington JM, Dinges DF. Sleep 2003;26:117-126. doi:10.1093/sleep/26.2.117.

CBT-I when sleep is actually broken

T1 · refer

Do: refer, or use a validated digital programme. Do not deliver CBT-I as coaching.

Nie S, et al. J Health Psychol 2026. doi:10.1177/13591053261461928. 7 RCTs, 4,751 participants; moderate-quality evidence that full CBT-I improves absenteeism and presenteeism.
Not this: VO2 max as "the strongest single predictor of all-cause mortality." That superlative is not in the literature. The largest cohort, 122,007 people, uses none.
MEMentalThe inner weather. Not the absence of illness, the presence of a good state.5›

Behavioural activation

T1 · pooled · SMD 0.67

Do: activity scheduling plus activity monitoring. Schedule valued action first and let mood follow, rather than waiting for motivation to return.

105 trials and 13,933 patients, forty of them at low risk of bias. It is behavioural and structural, which keeps it on the coaching side of the line. It is also the mechanism underneath what you already do by instinct when you get someone moving again.

Cuijpers P, Ciharova M, Tong L, et al. Clin Psychol Rev 2026;128:102783. doi:10.1016/j.cpr.2026.102783. SMD 0.67 (95% CI 0.54-0.80) versus control.

HRV biofeedback and slow paced breathing

T1 · g 0.83

Do: paced breathing at roughly six breaths per minute, daily.

Goessl VC, Curtiss JE, Hofmann SG. Psychol Med 2017;47:2578-2586. doi:10.1017/s0033291717001003. 24 studies, 484 participants, between-groups Hedges g = 0.83.

Cyclic sighing, five minutes

T2 · single study

Do: double inhale through the nose, long slow exhale through the mouth. Five minutes daily.

Randomized against box breathing, cyclic hyperventilation and mindfulness meditation over one month. The extended exhale produced the greatest improvement in mood and the largest drop in respiratory rate. Cheap, fast, low risk, and one trial.

Balban MY, Neri E, Kogon MM, et al. Cell Rep Med 2023;4:100895. doi:10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895. NCT05304000.

Worry postponement

T2 · randomized

Do: a designated thirty-minute worry window, early evening, never at bedtime. Outside the window, note it and defer it.

Versluis A, Verkuil B, Brosschot JF. Br J Health Psychol 2016;21:318-335. doi:10.1111/bjhp.12170. Randomized, 996 participants. Also Krzikalla C, et al. Clin Psychol Eur 2024;6:e12741.

Meditation, honestly scoped

T1 · with a ceiling

Say: it works, it is not magic, and it is not better than the other things on this list.

Moderate evidence of improvement in anxiety, depression and pain against an active control. No evidence it outperforms other active treatments such as exercise or medication.

Goyal M, Singh S, Sibinga EM, et al. JAMA Intern Med 2014;174:357-368. doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.13018.
Not this: gratitude journaling as a pillar. The 2024 PNAS meta-analysis, 145 papers and 24,804 participants, puts it at g = 0.19. A garnish, never load-bearing.
CGCognitiveAttention, memory and the machinery that carries them.2›

Sleep is the cognitive intervention

T1 · causal

Do: run the fourteen-day audit from Physical. The strongest cognitive-performance finding available is a sleep finding.

Van Dongen HP, et al. Sleep 2003;26:117-126. doi:10.1093/sleep/26.2.117.

Aerobic exercise for executive function, at honest size

T1 · small effect

Say: small but real. Promise a floor, not sharpness.

Ren FF, et al. Psychiatry Res 2026;359:117023. 10 RCTs, 780 adults: core executive function g = 0.14 (95% CI 0.05-0.22), inhibitory control g = 0.19. Also Ye M, et al. Int J Nurs Stud 2024;160:104912.
Not this, and saying so is the sale: commercial brain training. Simons DJ, Boot WR, Charness N, et al. Psychol Sci Public Interest 2016;17:103-186 is the definitive review, and Stojanoski B, et al. Neuropsychologia 2018;117:541-550 is titled "Converging evidence against the transferable benefits of online brain training on cognitive function." Executives buy these.
SXSexualDesire and function, which move with sleep and stress before they move with hormones.4›

Attention training against cognitive distraction

T1 · scope-sensitive

Do: name the mechanism and train presence. Refer for the clinical version.

Under chronic stress the predictor of reduced genital arousal was cognitive distraction, not hormones. The intervention built to target exactly that is mindfulness-based cognitive therapy for sexual interest and arousal disorder.

Mechanism: Hamilton LD, Meston CM. J Sex Med 2013;10:2443-2454. doi:10.1111/jsm.12249. Intervention: Brotto LA, Zdaniuk B. Arch Sex Behav 2025;54:3671-3682. doi:10.1007/s10508-025-03255-7. Pilot RCT: Thomas HN, et al. Arch Sex Behav 2025;54:221-230.
Scope line: MBCT for a sexual disorder is treatment. This domain is framed as stress physiology affecting intimacy, not sex therapy. The coach-deliverable version is attention and presence work plus naming the mechanism. The clinical version is a referral.

Aerobic exercise for erectile function

T1 · inside scope

Do: prescribe it, and name what it is for.

The best item this domain can own: physical, prescribable, never touches clinical sexual content, and carries randomized evidence in men with diagnosed dysfunction.

Silva AB, Sousa N, Azevedo LF, Martins C. Br J Sports Med 2017;51:1419-1424. doi:10.1136/bjsports-2016-096418. 7 RCTs, 478 participants. Activity levels: Gerbild H, et al. Sex Med 2018;6:75-89. doi:10.1016/j.esxm.2018.02.001.

Pelvic floor muscle training

T2 · refer

Do: refer to a pelvic floor physiotherapist. Do not coach it.

Ahmad AM, Abosteit EH, Said Kadah A, Draz RS. J Physiother 2026;72:122-127. doi:10.1016/j.jphys.2026.03.009. Three-arm randomized trial, concealed allocation, assessor blinding, 90 men.

Sleep, stated at its real size

T2 · small n

Say: "in a small laboratory study of young men." Never generalize it to a fifty-year-old as established fact.

One week of five-hour nights lowered daytime testosterone 10 to 15% in ten healthy young men. Cortisol did not change, so the drop was not cortisol-mediated, and it does not replicate at population scale.

Leproult R, Van Cauter E. JAMA 2011;305(21):2173-2174. doi:10.1001/jama.2011.710. n=10, mean age 24.3.
Not this: the cortisol-to-testosterone-to-dysfunction chain, contradicted at both ends. The 46.79% prevalence figure, which measures health care workers and argues the opposite of what it was cited for. And "45% never seek help," which is men and women aged 40 to 80 across nine Asian countries, and is "sought no help," a snapshot.

Treat one, both improve

T1 · causal

Do: never scope intimate or withdrawal work to the individual when he is partnered.

In a placebo-controlled trial, treating only the man produced significantly greater improvement in his untreated partner's satisfaction.

Heiman JR, et al. BJOG 2007;114(4):437-447. doi:10.1111/j.1471-0528.2006.01228.x. P<0.0001.

Couple therapy, by referral

T1 · g 1.12

Do: refer. What gets delivered here is the structured conversation and the decision to go.

Roddy MK, Walsh LM, Rothman K, Hatch SG, Doss BD. J Consult Clin Psychol 2020;88:583-596. doi:10.1037/ccp0000514. 58 studies, 2,092 couples, pre-post Hedges g = 1.12 (CI 0.92-1.31).

Attachment as context, not intervention

T1 · pooled

Attachment anxiety runs r = .42 against negative mental health; avoidance runs .28. The umbrella word "insecurity" is wrong. The ECR-R is already in the battery.

Zhang X, Li J, Xie F, et al. JPSP 2022;123(5):1089-1137. doi:10.1037/pspp0000437. 224 studies, 79,722 participants.
Do not claim: that withdrawal reads to a partner as rejection rather than as a stress response. No study tests it. Offer it as what you see in the room, never as a finding.
SCSocialThe wider circle. Its absence is measured in mortality, not mood.2›

Target the interpretation, not the calendar

T1 · pooled

Do: for the lonely executive the intervention is not more events. It is examining what he concludes about himself from the room he was just in.

Four strategies compared: social skills, social support, opportunities for contact, and maladaptive social cognition. Addressing maladaptive social cognition had the largest effect. Randomized comparisons yielded smaller effects than uncontrolled designs, which is worth saying out loud.

Masi CM, Chen HY, Hawkley LC, Cacioppo JT. Pers Soc Psychol Rev 2011;15:219-266. doi:10.1177/1088868310377394.

Social connection as a health variable

T1 · say it precisely

Say: "lacking social connection," and "up to" fifteen cigarettes. Holt-Lunstad corrects that drift herself.

Holt-Lunstad J, Smith TB, Layton JB. PLoS Med 2010;7(7):e1000316. doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.1000316. 148 studies, 308,849 participants.
Not this: "CEOs in structured peer groups report 71% improved company performance." Vendor marketing with no author, year or link. Vistage's own research has no such figure. Careful on any rewrite: a real, unrelated 71% exists (Korn Ferry 2024, CEO imposter syndrome).
FIFinancialSecurity, and the standing that comes with it.2›

Stop teaching, start automating

T1 · pooled

Do: defaults, automation and pre-commitment, plus just-in-time action at the decision point. Not a curriculum.

Interventions to improve financial literacy explain about 0.1% of the variance in the financial behaviours studied, with weaker effects in low-income samples, and negligible effects twenty months or more after instruction.

Fernandes D, Lynch JG, Netemeyer RG. Management Science 2014. Meta-analysis of 168 papers covering 201 studies.

Work the identity side, because that is the keystone

T2

Do: values affirmation before a consequential financial decision, not after it.

Zhang Y, Chen B, Hu X, Wang M. Am Psychol 2025. doi:10.1037/amp0001591. 129 tests from 67 articles.
Honest position: this is the thinnest intervention evidence base of the ten. The keystone claim is good. The toolkit is borrowed from behavioural economics rather than from clinical trials, and it should be presented that way.
Not this: "financial stress drives 67% of employees toward job changes." No US survey supports it. The nearest real figure is an Indian survey, n=637, asking a different question.
ENEnvironmentalThe rooms and the ground you spend your hours on.1›
The hard thing first. The 183-trial meta-analysis found nature-based interventions not significantly better than controls. The 120-minute finding is cross-sectional self-report, so the verb is "associated with," never "produces."

Circadian light hygiene

T2 · prospective, large

Do: bright light early, dark late. Measurable, environment-level, and the one item here carrying prospective evidence at scale.

Brighter light at night predicted higher incidence of coronary artery disease, myocardial infarction, heart failure, atrial fibrillation and stroke, independent of established risk factors.

Windred DP, Burns AC, Rutter MK, et al. JAMA Netw Open 2025;8:e2539031. doi:10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2025.39031. UK Biobank, roughly 13 million hours of wrist-sensor light data, about 89,000 adults, 9.5 years.
Not this: "biophilic design improves cognitive performance by 20%." No source. The likely origin is a 1999 consultancy report on daylit classrooms where students progressed 20% faster in maths.
SPSpiritualWhat the work is for, underneath the roles.2›

Purpose as a measurable health variable

T1 · 488,765 people

Do: treat purpose as a variable with an outcome literature, not as a retreat mood. The Flourishing Index is already in the battery.

Purpose in life associated with roughly 30% lower risk of earlier mortality across twenty-five samples and up to thirty-two years of follow-up.

Sutin A, Zavala D, Milad E, et al. Psychol Med 2026;56:e214. doi:10.1017/s0033291726104863. 488,765 participants, 48,928 deaths. Earlier: Cohen R, Bavishi C, Rozanski A. Psychosom Med 2016;78:122-133.

Values affirmation writing

T1 · modest

Do: ten minutes writing about a core personal value, ideally before a threat or evaluation rather than after it.

Zhang Y, Chen B, Hu X, Wang M. Am Psychol 2025. doi:10.1037/amp0001591.
Not this: "spiritual engagement is one of the strongest predictors of flourishing across 22 countries." The study measures religious service attendance and its authors decline the spirituality framing; they say "most consistently associated," not strongest, at 0.27, mid-pack; and Kenya, South Africa and Tanzania ran negative.

Do: the same ten-minute protocol as Spiritual. This is where it belongs most, because it is the best-evidenced practice for the domain that determines the others.

Zhang Y, et al. Am Psychol 2025. doi:10.1037/amp0001591.

Job crafting

T2 · correlational

Say: meta-analytic but correlational. A good frame, not a proven treatment.

Wang H, Li P, Chen S. Int J Environ Res Public Health 2020;17:8016. doi:10.3390/ijerph17218016. 51 studies, 54 samples, N=17,863.

Concealment, as assessment rather than exercise

T1 · pooled

Situational concealment and dispositional concealment are not interchangeable constructs, and the literature treats them separately.

Pachankis JE, et al. Psychol Bull 2020;146(10):831-871. doi:10.1037/bul0000271. 193 studies, N=92,236, ESr = 0.116. Also Larson et al. 2015, 92 studies, depression r+ = .40, authenticity r+ = -.47. Pachankis 2007 is a conceptual review and cannot carry an effect size.
Not this: any sentence of the form "women cover more than men." Gender moderation is null, p = 0.732. And never introduce the 67% covering figure with "research shows": it is a Deloitte consultancy survey pooling C-suite, other executives and senior managers, and the publisher has to be named.

Ten situations

the playbooks

The domains are the library. These are the pages you open under pressure.

The board meeting is in ten minutes and the heart is going

ME · CG
Right now
Five minutes of cyclic sighing, extended exhale. Then arousal reappraisal: name the pounding heart as the body delivering oxygen for performance, not as a warning.
Standing on
Balban 2023, randomized. Reappraisal: Bosshard M, Gomez P. Sci Rep 2024;14:7923, 44 effect sizes, d = 0.23, small but significant and larger for mixed interventions.
Trap
Power posing. The postural-feedback effect is unreliable and the confident version did not replicate.

The board meeting is tomorrow and he cannot sleep

PH · ME
Tonight
Do not try harder. Move the worry to a designated window earlier in the evening, and protect time in bed rather than chasing sleep.
This quarter
If it is chronic rather than situational, CBT-I. Digital is fine and it is first-line.
Trap
Alcohol as a sleep aid, and treating one bad night as evidence of a sleep disorder.

Single, feeling unattractive, sex life at zero, and up for a big promotion

SX · ID · FI
This week
Aerobic exercise, prescribed as a sexual-function intervention and named as such. Plus behavioural activation against the withdrawal, because withdrawal is what is actually compounding.
This quarter
Values affirmation before the promotion decision, to decouple worth from the outcome. This is Identity Fusion doing exactly what the keystone predicts.
Standing on
Silva 2017 (7 RCTs, 478 participants). Cuijpers 2026 (SMD 0.67). Zhang 2025.
Trap
Ordering a testosterone panel as the first move. The hormonal chain is contradicted, coaches do not order labs, and a normal result lands as "so it is just me," which is the worst available outcome here.

Using food for comfort in a peak-stress stretch, now feeling fat and sluggish

PH · ME
First
Check sleep, because it reframes everything. Sleep curtailment lowered leptin, raised ghrelin, and increased hunger and appetite. At five hours a night this is not a willpower failure, it is appetite regulation with a known cause.
This week
Implementation intentions on the exact trigger moment, written down. "If it is 4pm and I am reaching for the drawer, then I fill my water bottle and walk one lap."
Standing on
Spiegel K, Tasali E, Penev P, Van Cauter E. Ann Intern Med 2004;141:846-850, randomized crossover. Gollwitzer and Sheeran 2006, 94 tests, 8,000+ participants, d = 0.65.
Trap
Dieting harder while still sleeping five hours. That is fighting a hormone with a rule.

Role loss, layoff or exit, and the identity goes with it

ID · ME · SP
Week one
Behavioural activation. Structure and scheduled valued action, before motivation returns rather than after.
This quarter
Values affirmation writing, plus purpose work with a real instrument.
Trap
A financial literacy course as the fix. It explains 0.1% of behaviour variance.

Two hundred contacts and no one to call

SC
The move
Do not add events. Work the interpretation. Addressing maladaptive social cognition outperformed social skills, social support, and increased contact opportunities.
Standing on
Masi 2011, the four-strategy comparison.
Trap
Joining another peer group on the promise of a 71% performance lift. That number is vendor marketing.

Running on five to six hours and saying "I function fine"

PH · CG
The move
A fourteen-day time-in-bed extension with an objective performance measure. At six hours for fourteen days, deficits matched two nights of total deprivation while subjective sleepiness plateaued.
Trap
Trusting how he feels. The study exists precisely because self-report fails here.

His partner says he is not present, and he is withdrawing at home

RL · SX
The move
Treat it as a dyad. Improve one person and the untreated partner's satisfaction improves measurably. Refer to couple therapy where indicated.
Standing on
Heiman 2007 (176 couples, causal). Roddy 2020 (g = 1.12).
Trap
Solving it alone, and asserting that his partner reads withdrawal as rejection. That specific claim is untested. Offer it as what you see in the room.

Stuck in a rumination loop after a bad review or a conflict

ME · CG
Right now
Distanced self-talk. Address himself by name and in the second or third person. It reduces negative emotional reactivity without the cognitive cost of reappraisal, which matters because reappraisal is expensive exactly when he is depleted.
This week
The worry postponement window.
Standing on
Webster CT, Berg MK, Kross E, Moser JS. Int J Psychophysiol 2022;177:122-132, a direct replication comparing it against detached reappraisal.
Trap
"Just stop thinking about it," and venting repeatedly, which rehearses rather than resolves.

A money crisis, while still having to lead

FI · CG · ID
This week
Implementation intentions on the single next financial action, not the whole plan. Behavioural activation to hold function up while the stressor persists.
This quarter
Automation and defaults over education. Values affirmation to stop the crisis becoming a verdict on the self, which is exactly what Identity Fusion predicts it will do.
The edge
Cognitive to Financial is a strength-3 connection in the model: sustained focus is upstream of revenue. This is that edge under load.
Trap
Financial education as the intervention, and letting the money story become the identity story unchallenged.
